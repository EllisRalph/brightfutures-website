In today's classrooms, educators face growing pressure to support diverse learners while meeting rigorous academic standards. One of the most effective tools we have is formative assessment: a continuous cycle of checking for understanding, giving feedback and adjusting teaching.

Unlike traditional assessment, formative assessment isn't about grading. It's about gathering insight. Quick checks such as exit tickets, peer review, mini-quizzes or a simple "show me" gesture can reveal misconceptions early and guide teaching in real time.

What it helps teachers do

Used consistently, formative assessment helps teachers:

  • Differentiate their teaching more effectively
  • Identify learning gaps before they become learning failures
  • Empower pupils to take ownership of their progress
  • Build a classroom culture focused on growth rather than perfection

From one-off event to continuous journey

With the right strategies, formative assessment turns learning from a one-off event into a continuous journey, for pupils and teachers alike.
