DBS Update Service

Keep your DBS up to date, and take it with you

By subscribing to the DBS Update Service, your DBS certificate stays up to date and can be carried over when you change jobs or roles in the same sector, so you don't need a new check every time.

What to do now

  • {{icon:check}}Subscribe to the Update Service. It costs £16 a year.
  • {{icon:check}}You can sign up as soon as the DBS starts processing your application.
  • {{icon:check}}Keep a note of your certificate number and subscription ID to share with us.
{{icon:clock}}

Don't miss the deadline. You can only subscribe within 30 days of your certificate being issued.

Free resources

Free resources for your classroom

Trusted, free sites we recommend to the teachers and support staff we work with.

  • Oak National Academy {{icon:external}}A large library of free online lessons and resources from Reception to Year 11, useful for planning, remote learning or extra materials.
  • BBC Bitesize {{icon:external}}Free study support across many subjects and a wide age range, ideal for in-class use and revision.
  • Primary Resources {{icon:external}}Downloadable lesson plans, worksheets and activity ideas for EYFS, KS1 and KS2.
  • Teachit {{icon:external}}Teacher-shared resources including worksheets, lesson ideas, quizzes and games for primary classes.
  • EC Publishing {{icon:external}}Free PSHE and RSHE schemes of work and lesson plans, helpful for pastoral and wellbeing teaching.
  • Swyft Resources {{icon:external}}Free lesson bundles, worksheets and activity packs for primary, secondary and sixth form.
  • iTutorStore {{icon:external}}Free cross-curricular resources for science, history, PE, drama and more.
  • Childnet International {{icon:external}}Free resources on online safety, cyberbullying and digital wellbeing for staff working with pupils.
