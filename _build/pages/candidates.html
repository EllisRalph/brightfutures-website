What you'll need

Getting compliant, made easy

Every candidate goes through the same rigorous checks, so schools can trust that the people we send are safe and suitable. We'll explain exactly what we need and help you gather it.

  • {{icon:check}}An enhanced DBS, ideally on the Update Service (we can help you apply)
  • {{icon:check}}References covering at least the last two years
  • {{icon:check}}Proof of identity and your right to work in the UK
  • {{icon:check}}Your qualifications and relevant training certificates
