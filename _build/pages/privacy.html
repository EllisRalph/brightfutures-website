Who we are

Bright Futures Recruitment ("we", "us") is an education recruitment agency. We are the data controller for the personal information we collect through this website and in the course of our recruitment services. You can contact us about anything in this policy by emailing {{EMAIL}} or calling {{PHONE}}.

Information we collect

If you're a candidate

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number and location.
  • Your CV, work history, qualifications, training and references.
  • Information needed for safeguarding and compliance checks, including identity documents, right to work, enhanced DBS and barred list checks, Teaching Regulation Agency checks and, where applicable, overseas police checks.
  • Your preferences about the type of work, hours and locations you're interested in.

If you're a school or client

  • The names, job titles and contact details of the people we deal with, and details of the roles you need to fill.

Website visitors

  • Anything you send us through a form on this website. This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use your information

  • To find you suitable work, and to introduce you to schools, settings and homes with your agreement.
  • To carry out the safeguarding and vetting checks required by Keeping Children Safe in Education.
  • To arrange payment for your work, including through our umbrella company partner.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services to schools.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Our lawful bases

We process personal information where it is necessary to take steps towards, or to perform, a contract with you; where we have a legal obligation (for example right to work and safeguarding checks); where it is in our legitimate interests to run a recruitment business; and, where required, with your consent. Criminal record information is processed only as permitted by law for roles working with children.

Who we share it with

We share candidate information with schools, settings and homes you are being considered for, with referees, with the Disclosure and Barring Service and other checking bodies, with our umbrella company partner for payroll, and with trusted IT and service providers who help us run our business. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet legal requirements, and we review it regularly. You can ask us to delete your details at any time, subject to any records we must keep by law.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To make a request, email {{EMAIL}}.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Keeping your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
