Our philosophy

The right people in the right places

We believe education has the power to transform lives, and that begins with the right people in the right places. Our philosophy is simple: we're dedicated to connecting passionate, talented educators with the schools and communities that need them most.

We don't just fill vacancies; we build lasting relationships. By taking the time to understand both our candidates and our partner schools, we make sure every placement is the right fit, for today and for the future.

We're here to support educators at every stage of their career, while helping schools build strong, safe learning communities.

Safeguarding

Integrity and excellence, in everything we do

We uphold the highest standards of compliance, carrying out rigorous checks and vetting to protect the safety and wellbeing of every child. Our commitment goes beyond meeting requirements: it's about fostering trust and creating environments where students, staff and schools can thrive.

Where we work

Local roots, national reach

We proudly partner with all types of schools across the UK, from Kent, Essex and London to the Midlands and the North: primary, secondary, SEMH and SEND, alternative provision and independent schools, as well as therapeutic schools and children's residential homes.

Our specialist SEND division is dedicated to supporting schools and professionals who work with children and young people with additional needs.
