Vetting and safeguarding

The safety of every child comes first

We follow the latest Department for Education guidance, Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE), so every candidate we put forward meets the highest safeguarding standards.

{{icon:file}}For your peace of mind, schools receive a detailed vetting form confirming every check we've completed on each candidate.

How we vet candidates {{arrow}}

  • {{icon:check}}Comprehensive background and identity checks
  • {{icon:check}}Right to work verification
  • {{icon:check}}Enhanced DBS and barred list checks
  • {{icon:check}}Teaching Regulation Agency (TRA) and Education Workforce Council (EWC) checks
  • {{icon:check}}Overseas police checks, where applicable
  • {{icon:check}}Verification of qualifications
  • {{icon:check}}References covering recent employment
  • {{icon:check}}Assessment of professional suitability

Our commitment

Brighter futures start with the right people in the right places

At Bright Futures Recruitment we're passionate about supporting education professionals at every stage of their journey. Our mission is to help you find work that matches your skills and ambitions, and lets you thrive and make a real difference.

We partner with all types of schools, from Essex, Kent and London to the Midlands and the North. Our specialist SEND division supports schools and professionals who work with children and young people with additional needs.
