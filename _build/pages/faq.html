Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered

How do I register with the agency? {{icon:plus}}

Call the team on {{PHONE}}, email {{EMAIL}} or send us your CV online, and we'll send you our registration forms. We'll then arrange a short phone call and request the documents we need for compliance.

Do I need a DBS, and do I need to be on the Update Service? {{icon:plus}}

Yes, you'll need an enhanced DBS. If you don't have one, we can help you apply. Being on the DBS Update Service is preferred, as it keeps your certificate current and portable. Find out more about the Update Service.

What schools do you work with? {{icon:plus}}

We partner with primary, secondary, SEMH and SEND, alternative provision and independent schools across our local area and the wider UK, as well as therapeutic schools and children's residential homes.

How often will I get paid? {{icon:plus}}

Our workers are paid weekly through an umbrella company we partner with. Some long-term or permanent roles may follow the school's own payroll.

What support do you offer once I'm placed in a school? {{icon:plus}}

We stay in regular contact to make sure you feel supported. You'll have a dedicated consultant, and access to CPD, feedback and ongoing guidance.

What types of staff do you supply? {{icon:plus}}

We provide a full range of education professionals, including:

  • {{icon:check}}Teachers (primary, secondary and SEND)
  • {{icon:check}}Teaching assistants and learning support assistants
  • {{icon:check}}SEND specialists
  • {{icon:check}}SENCOs
  • {{icon:check}}Cover supervisors
  • {{icon:check}}Therapists (SALT, OT and more)
  • {{icon:check}}Pastoral and behaviour support staff
  • {{icon:check}}Middle and senior leaders
Are your candidates fully vetted? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. Every candidate goes through rigorous compliance checks, including:

  • {{icon:check}}Enhanced DBS (on the Update Service where possible)
  • {{icon:check}}Full referencing covering at least two years
  • {{icon:check}}Right to work verification
  • {{icon:check}}Qualification and relevant training checks
  • {{icon:check}}Safeguarding verification

We follow Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE) guidelines at all times.

Do you cover long-term and permanent roles? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. We support day-to-day, long-term and permanent placements, and can also offer temp to perm arrangements.
