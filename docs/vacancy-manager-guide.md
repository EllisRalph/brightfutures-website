# Managing your vacancies

Your website has a simple vacancy manager. You can add, edit and remove jobs yourself, from any computer, phone or tablet.

## Logging in
1. Go to **brightfuturesrecruitment.com/admin**
2. Click **Login** and sign in with your email address and password.
   (The first time, you'll get an email invitation. Click the link in it to set your password.)
3. Forgotten your password? Click **Login**, then **Forgot password?**

## Adding a vacancy
1. Click **Vacancies**, then the **+ Vacancy** button.
2. Fill in the boxes:
   - **Job title**, for example *SEMH Teaching Assistant*
   - **Role type**: pick from the list (this puts the job in the right category)
   - **Location**: town and county, for example *Basildon, Essex*
   - **Area**: used by the search filter on the jobs page
   - **Contract type**, **Hours**, **Start date** and **Pay**. Leave pay blank if you'd rather not show it.
   - **Short summary**: one or two sentences. This shows on the job card and in Google.
   - **Full job description**: type or paste the advert. Use **Heading 2** for section titles (such as *The role*, *About the school*, *What you'll need*, *What's on offer*) and bullet points for lists.
   - **Date posted**: today's date. Newest jobs show first.
   - **Closing date** (optional): after this date the job is marked as closed.
3. The right-hand side shows a preview of how the job page will look.
4. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**. Your job is live on the website in about a minute.

## Editing a vacancy
Click **Vacancies**, click the job, make your changes, then **Publish** and **Publish now**.

## Taking a vacancy down
- **Filled, but you might reuse the advert later?** Open it, switch off **Show on website** and publish. It stays in your list (use **Filter by > Hidden** to find it) and you can switch it back on any time.
- **Gone for good?** Open it and click **Delete entry**.

## Featuring a vacancy on the homepage
Switch on **Feature on the homepage**. Featured jobs appear first in the homepage's *Latest vacancies*.

## Testimonials
Click **Testimonials** to add kind words from candidates and schools. They show on the Testimonials page, and the newest two appear on the homepage. Only publish someone's name if they're happy for you to.
